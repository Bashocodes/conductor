# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and
  learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, sexual attention, or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of
acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct,
and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the
maintainers through the repository's private security-reporting channel:
<https://github.com/Bashocodes/conductor/security/advisories/new>.

All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect
the reporter's privacy and safety.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these community-impact guidelines:

1. **Correction:** A private written warning and clarification for
   inappropriate language or unprofessional behavior.
2. **Warning:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior and a
   temporary restriction on interaction.
3. **Temporary ban:** A time-limited ban from interaction or public
   communication with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** Permanent removal for a pattern of violations,
   harassment, or aggression.
